What it gets right

The sugar number is the standout. 0.5 g per gummy and 2.5 g across a full serving is genuinely low here. Plenty of competitors run 1–3 g per gummy, which turns a six-gummy dose into 6–18 g of added sugar a day. If you are taking this daily for six months, that difference is not trivial.

Taste is the most repeated theme across more than 1,500 customer reviews, with 90% at five stars. That sounds like a soft metric until you remember what you are actually buying with a gummy: the probability that you take it every day. Creatine works through accumulation, so palatability is the mechanism, not a bonus.

Focused product line. SWOLY was built around the gummy rather than bolting gummies onto an existing powder business, and the formulation reflects that.

The gap

SWOLY states its gummies are third-party tested for accuracy, purity and potency. We have no reason to doubt it — but that claim is the brand's own, and SWOLY did not appear in either of the two public independent testing rounds. In a category where an independent lab found four of six gummies underdosed, and where one brand advertising its own certified results failed twice, a self-reported claim is not the same class of evidence as NSF Certified for Sport.

What to do: email SWOLY and ask for the certificate of analysis for the batch you would be shipped. Any brand confident in its product will send one, and the speed of the reply tells you something on its own.

The other trade-offs

  • Five gummies per dose versus Create's three. Manageable, but more chewing every morning.
  • 4 g per serving sits at the lower end of the 3–5 g research range. Fine in practice, worth knowing.
  • One flavour (strawberry). Not an issue unless you dislike it.

Who should buy it

Buy it if you have abandoned creatine before because of taste, and you want the lowest sugar load available in a gummy. On those two axes it is the best thing we have found.

Choose Create instead if independent verification is what is holding you back — NSF Certified for Sport plus a published lab measurement is a materially stronger evidence base.

Questions

Are SWOLY creatine gummies third-party tested?

SWOLY states its gummies are tested by independent labs for accuracy, purity and potency, and that they are made in a GMP-certified facility. However SWOLY did not appear in either of the two public testing rounds we track, so there is no independent published result to point at. We would ask for a batch certificate of analysis before committing to a subscription.

How many SWOLY gummies is a serving?

Five gummies, giving 4 g of creatine monohydrate and 2.5 g of sugar.

How much sugar is in SWOLY creatine gummies?

0.5 g per gummy, so 2.5 g across a full five-gummy serving. That is low for the category, where a full dose can carry 6–18 g.

Is SWOLY good value?

A tub is 150 gummies, or 30 full servings, so $32.98 works out at about $1.10 per 4 g serving — roughly $1.37 per 5 g equivalent, dropping to about $1.16 on subscription. That is the low end of the gummy market and still several times the price of powder.
