Questions

Is Legion Creatine Gummies NSF Certified for Sport?

Legion states its gummies are made in an NSF-certified facility and tested by an ISO 17025-accredited lab. That is a meaningfully different claim from a product-level "NSF Certified for Sport" listing in NSF's public registry. Search the registry yourself for this exact product and flavor before relying on certification as your deciding factor.

How much creatine is in a serving of Legion gummies?

5 g of creatine monohydrate per serving, per the brand's current label — the same standard dose used in most creatine research, and higher than several competitors that dose at 4–4.5 g per serving.

Is Legion cheaper than Create?

On a price-per-gram basis, yes — Legion is priced competitively for the category. Create's premium reflects its confirmed NSF Certified for Sport status and a published independent lab measurement, which Legion does not have publicly available at time of writing.
