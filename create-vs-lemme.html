Where Create comes out ahead

  • Published test data. Create has a measured gram figure from an ISO 17025 lab (4.59 g against 4.5 g) plus a second passing round. Lemme’s certification is real, but there is no public potency number to check it against.
  • Cost and reordering. About $1.78 per 5 g on subscription against about $2.00, and a 30-serving tub instead of a 15-serving bottle, so half as many reorders.
  • Fewer pieces. Three gummies a day instead of four.

Where Lemme comes out ahead

  • Full 5 g per serving versus Create’s 4.5 g. Both sit inside the 3–5 g daily range studies use, so the gap matters less than it looks.
  • Slightly less sugar per serving on the NSF records we checked (4 g vs 5 g).

Which should you buy?

If you want the most public evidence per dollar, Create. If you specifically want a full 5 g in a certified gummy and don’t mind the higher price and smaller bottles, Lemme is a reasonable pick. Either way, match the flavor you buy to its NSF listing.
