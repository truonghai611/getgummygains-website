What's actually new here

Create's launch email, titled "ICYMI: Try the Lemonade Collection NOW," announces four lemonade-flavored additions to its existing Creatine + Electrolytes stick-pack line: Classic Lemonade, Peach Lemonade, Raspberry Lemon, and Strawberry Lemon, sold individually or as a Variety Lemonade pack. These join the product's existing flavors — Lemon Lime, Watermelon, and Passionfruit — which we already cover on our best creatine electrolyte mix page.

This is a flavor expansion, not a reformulation. Per Create's own product page, the underlying supplement facts are unchanged from the stick pack we already track:

  • 5 g creatine monohydrate per stick — the full research-backed dose, not diluted to make room for the electrolyte blend
  • 800 mg electrolyte blend (sodium, potassium, magnesium)
  • 1,000 mg taurine, included for hydration and exercise-tolerance support — not a stimulant
  • Zero sugar and zero caffeine, sweetened with OnoSweet (95% Reb-M, a stevia-derived sweetener) rather than sugar or artificial dyes
  • Vegan, mixed with 8–12 oz of water per stick

How we read it. If you already use Create's Creatine + Electrolytes and simply wanted more flavor variety, this is a straightforward, low-risk addition — the dose you're relying on has not changed. If you were on the fence about the product itself, the launch doesn't add new evidence either way; the formula and its trade-offs are the same ones we cover on our creatine + electrolytes buying guide.

Certifications: what the launch claims, and what to verify

Create's official site lists NSF Certified for Sport across its core product range, which is an ongoing third-party programme with a public, searchable registry — the strongest verification standard we track on this site. Create's Lemonade Collection launch marketing separately highlights Eurofins-tested quality and an Informed Sport-style certification claim on its promotional graphics.

We treat certification claims as specific to a SKU and lot, not as a blanket brand guarantee. A new flavor can launch before it individually appears in a public certification registry. Before buying, check the actual label on the box you'd receive, or search the NSF Certified for Sport registry for the exact product name, the same way we recommend on our Create gummies review.

Price and the LEMONADE launch code

At the time of writing, Create lists the Lemonade Collection at $58 for a one-time 30-count box, or $48/month on subscription (20% off a $60 list price). A 60-count, two-person subscription box runs $92/month. Create's launch email referenced a LEMONADE promo code tied to the collection's release.

Treat all of this as a snapshot, not a guarantee: supplement pricing, subscription discounts, and promo codes change frequently, and a code mentioned in a launch email can expire. Confirm the live price and whether a code still applies at checkout rather than trusting the numbers on this page.

Who should care about this launch

Worth trying if you already buy Create's Creatine + Electrolytes and want more flavor variety, or you've avoided electrolyte-creatine combos before because they taste like unflavored sports-drink powder — a citrus-forward lemonade profile is a genuinely different taste category from the brand's existing Watermelon or Passionfruit options.

Skip it if you don't currently need an electrolyte product on top of your creatine. As we cover in our electrolyte combo guide, the combined format only earns its price on long, hot, or high-sweat training days — a new flavor doesn't change that math. On a normal day, a plain gummy, stick, or powder does the same creatine job for less.

Questions

What flavors are in Create's Lemonade Collection?

Four flavors: Classic Lemonade, Peach Lemonade, Raspberry Lemon, and Strawberry Lemon, plus a Variety Lemonade pack that includes all three fruit flavors. They join Create's existing Creatine + Electrolytes flavors (Lemon Lime, Watermelon, Passionfruit).

Is the Lemonade Collection a new formula or just new flavors?

Just new flavors. Per Create's own product listing, the core formula is unchanged from the existing Creatine + Electrolytes stick: 5 g creatine monohydrate, an 800 mg sodium-potassium-magnesium electrolyte blend, and 1,000 mg taurine per stick, with zero sugar and zero caffeine, sweetened with OnoSweet (Reb-M).

How much does the Create Lemonade Collection cost?

At the time of writing, Create lists a 30-count box at $58 one-time or $48/month on subscription (20% off a $60 list price), and a 60-count two-person box at $92/month. Create's launch email also referenced a LEMONADE promo code. Prices and codes change, so confirm the current total at checkout rather than trusting any number here, including ours.

Is Create's Creatine + Electrolytes third-party certified?

Create's site lists NSF Certified for Sport across its product line, and the brand's Lemonade Collection launch materials also cite Eurofins-tested quality and an Informed Sport-style certification claim. We treat certification as SKU-specific: check the actual label or NSF's public registry for the exact flavor and lot you are buying rather than assuming a brand-wide badge covers every new flavor on day one.

Should I switch from Create's gummies to the Lemonade Collection?

Not automatically. The gummies and the Creatine + Electrolytes sticks are different products for different situations. The sticks make sense on long, hot, or high-sweat training days when you would be buying electrolytes separately anyway; on an average day the gummies (or plain powder) deliver the same creatine without an electrolyte load you may not need.
