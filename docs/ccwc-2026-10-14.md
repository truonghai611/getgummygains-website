# GetGummyGains: CCWC and conversion implementation

Editorial implementation: October 14, 2026. Framework supplied by the owner: Customer–Context–Why–Conversion. Segment hypotheses below come from page intent and editorial analysis, not a new audience survey or live analytics export.

| Customer / context | Why use GummyGains | Conversion path |
|---|---|---|
| Adult comparing formats after finding mixing inconvenient | Understand convenience versus cost and ingredients | Home → gummies vs powder → comparison or calculator |
| Skeptical buyer checking a lab-test claim | Separate a historical sample result from exact-product certification | Lab timeline → comparison → brand review |
| Cost-conscious shopper comparing packs and discounts | See complete labeled servings and recurring purchase terms | Calculator → pack comparison → merchant |
| Buyer already considering Create | Resolve label, ingredient, packaging and purchase questions | Create review → price/checklist → Create |
| First-time buyer with ingredient preferences | Read format and ingredient guidance before choosing | Women's/beginner guides → format comparison → evidence |

WHY: Help readers choose a creatine format and product with evidence, ingredients, serving economics and purchase commitments in view.
HOW: Dated public-source comparisons, archived product-label photographs, explicit commercial disclosure, current-storefront checks with limitations and a labeled-serving calculator.
WHAT: A research/comparison publisher, not a supplement shop, laboratory, clinician or seller-controlled review service.

## Shipped changes

- Home hero states reader outcome and offers comparison, rather than a universal verified winner.
- Replaced the illustrative lab certificate in the hero with the existing dated retail-label photograph and its scope.
- Four buying-context routes appear before the testing chart; publisher role, author, methodology and affiliate relationship are visible nearby.
- Home sticky link routes to decisions instead of taking an undecided reader straight to the merchant.
- Six core guides get a short context/fit/next-step summary with disclosures. Three purchase guides get a checkout checklist and seller responsibility explanation.
- Existing historical tests and prices keep their dates. October 14 Blue Raspberry product copy describes 1.5 g per gummy and 4.5 g per three gummies, while Create's homepage says 5 g per three gummies. The review explains that this storefront discrepancy is unresolved and requires checking the chosen pack. No universal new dose is inferred.
- Removed the claim that gummies necessarily improve adherence in the powder comparison, and the suggestion to add a fourth gummy to match a price-comparison unit.
- Navigation now says How we evaluate rather than How we test.
- Quiz answer state is available to assistive technology; responsive actions wrap on narrow screens.
- GA4 buyer_intent_click and decision_module_view events added alongside existing affiliate_outbound_click attribution. These do not measure sales or collect medical answers.

## Measure after deployment

Compare 14–28 days against a comparable prior period, separating landing page, traffic source and device. Track route clicks / page views; review-to-price-guide progression; affiliate clicks / relevant page sessions; and confirmed merchant orders / affiliate clicks when the affiliate dashboard supplies matching data. Exclude owner testing. Small samples and source mix can dominate the result. No conversion uplift has been measured or promised by this edit.
