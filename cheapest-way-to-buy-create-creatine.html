Serving and certification

The product description states 1.5 g of creatine monohydrate per gummy, or 4.5 g in three gummies. The NSF Blue Raspberry record lists a three-gummy serving, 5 g total sugar and 60 calories. Check the exact product label you receive; these facts are not a dosing instruction for every reader.

Why the homepage price can look different

Create’s homepage currently promotes gummy pouches alongside tubs, bundles and creatine with electrolytes. A starting price for one format is not the price of every format. Select the package, flavor, duration and purchase type before comparing.

Among the four options above, the three-month tub has the lowest displayed subscription cost per labeled serving. The one-month tub commits less money up front.
