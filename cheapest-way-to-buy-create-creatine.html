What is the cheapest way to buy Create creatine?

Create sells the same creatine in five different shapes at five different prices, and the cheapest one is not the one the shop pushes. We read every price from Create's own live product data on 13 September 2026 and put them all on a per-serving basis.

The headline number: the cheapest creatine Create sells is the unflavoured stick packs at about $0.85 per 5 g serving on subscription through our link. The cheapest gummy is not on the main menu at all — it is a legacy 45-count tub, three for $69.99, which works out to about $1.24 a serving. The 30-count single-serve pouch box is the most expensive way to buy the identical product, at about $1.55.

The $1 single-serve packet is a sampler, not a supply route — you cannot sensibly buy thirty of them. It is listed here because it is the cheapest way to find out whether you like a flavour before committing to a box.

Why the pouch box costs more for the same thirty servings

Create counts the tub in gummies and the pouch box in sachets. A 90-count tub is thirty three-gummy servings; a 30-count pouch box is thirty single-serve sachets. Both are one month. The pouch costs $12.50 more for the packaging, which is a reasonable price for travel convenience and a poor one for a kitchen cupboard.

Worth knowing: Create's own FAQ describes the 30-count pouch as a ten-day supply. Its bundle pricing contradicts that — the three-month pouch bundle is 90 count, which only works if 30 count is one month. We have gone with the bundle maths.

The 45-count tub that is still on sale but not on the menu

Create's main navigation offers two sizes. A third still exists: a 45-count tub at $27.99, sold in ones, twos and threes on a legacy product page. At three tubs for $69.99 that is 45 servings, or about $1.24 a serving through our link — the lowest gummy price Create offers, roughly 3% below subscribing to the 90-count tub and well below any bundle.

The catch is stock. Flavour availability on that page moves around; Orange has been the reliable one. If your flavour is out, the 90-count subscription is the next cheapest route and it comes with free shipping.

Shipping changes the answer on small orders

Every subscription order ships free. One-time orders need to reach $100 before shipping is free, which a single tub does not. On a one-tub order, shipping can wipe out the difference between the formats above, so compare the cart total rather than the product price.

Bottom line. If you want creatine as cheaply as Create sells it and do not care about flavour, the unflavoured stick packs at about $0.85 a serving are not close to being beaten. If you want the gummies specifically, subscribe to the 90-count tub and check the legacy 45-count page first — three tubs there is cheaper still when it is in stock.

Questions

What is the cheapest Create creatine per serving?

The unflavoured Creatine Monohydrate Stick Packs. A 30-count box lists at $40, is $32 on subscription, and comes to about $25.60 through our link — roughly $0.85 per 5 g serving. That is about a third of the price of Create's gummies for the same creatine.

What is the cheapest way to buy the gummies specifically?

Three of the legacy 45-count tubs for $69.99, which is 45 servings and works out to about $1.24 a serving through our link. It sits on an older product page that Create's navigation no longer links to, and flavour stock varies. Failing that, subscribe to the 90-count tub: $48 subscribed, about $38.40 through our link, or roughly $1.28 a serving with free shipping.

Is the 30-count pouch box worse value than the tub?

Yes, for the same quantity. Both hold thirty servings of the identical gummy; the pouch box is $72.50 against the tub's $60. You are paying $12.50 for thirty single-serve sachets, which is worth it for travel and not worth it at home.

Can I try a flavour before buying a box?

Create sells single-serve packets at $1 each in seven gummy flavours and three electrolyte flavours. Given that Create does not accept returns on its gummies even unopened, spending a dollar first is the sensible way to settle the flavour question.

Do these prices include shipping?

No. Subscription orders ship free; one-time orders need a cart of $100 or more. On a single-tub one-time order, shipping can be larger than the gap between two of the formats in the table, so compare the total at checkout.

How often do these prices change?

Often enough that a dated table is the only honest kind. Create changed its main pack sizes during 2026, which moved every per-serving figure on this site. We re-read Create's live product data monthly and stamp the date on every price we publish.
