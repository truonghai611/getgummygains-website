Certification and a lab report answer different questions

Certification can be checked against an ongoing public program. A laboratory result measures the submitted sample using a stated method. Read the date, exact product and scope of either source. A missing public certificate does not establish that a product is unsafe or that it failed a test.

How to verify your purchase

  1. Open the linked record or search NSF.
  2. Match brand, full product name, gummy format and flavor.
  3. Check the lot field and label against the product you receive.
  4. Read the full serving, sugar and current price before deciding.

Start with the comparison table, then review historical sample tests. For Create, compare tub and pouch prices. More registry entries do not by themselves mean a better product.

Questions

Are these the only certified creatine gummies?

No exhaustive claim is made here. These are four specific examples verified in the public directory on September 24, 2026. Search the live registry for other products and later changes.

Does a brand logo cover every flavor?

No. Use the exact product record and check the flavor, form and lot information. A brand-level badge is not enough to match a particular purchase.

Does certification erase an earlier failed lab result?

No. Keep both records with their dates and sample details. A historical result and a current registry listing address different evidence and cannot be substituted for each other.
