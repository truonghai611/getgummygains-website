How to cancel an OMNI Creatine subscription (and get a refund)

The short path: portal.omnicreatine.com → sign in with your order email → cancel. Below are the exact refund rules from OMNI’s own policy, what to do about a charge after cancelling, and the lookalike sites to avoid.

By Henry Nguyen · Updated November 24, 2026 · Source: OMNI refund policy (last updated November 4, 2026)

Disclosure: GummyGains is not affiliated with OMNI and cannot cancel or refund anything for you. We earn a commission if you buy Create through a link on this page. That does not change the steps below, which come from OMNI’s published policy. Full policy.

The short answer. Cancel any time at portal.omnicreatine.com. OMNI says no reason is needed and the cancellation takes effect right away, so you are not charged again. If the portal will not load, email support@omnicreatine.com from the address on your order; OMNI says it makes the change within 1 business day. An order that was already placed cannot be cancelled, but unopened product can be returned within 30 days of delivery.

Cancel in five steps

  1. Open the portal: go to portal.omnicreatine.com, or click the manage-order link in any OMNI order confirmation email (OMNI’s subscription policy points to those emails).
  2. Sign in with the email you used at checkout on omnicreatine.com.
  3. Open the active subscription and choose cancel. Pause and skip are also offered if you only want a break. No reason is required.
  4. Save proof: screenshot the cancelled status and keep the confirmation email. You will need it if a charge shows up later.
  5. Portal not working? Email support@omnicreatine.com from the address on your order, include the order number, and ask them to cancel. OMNI says it does this within 1 business day.

Timing tip: an order that has already been placed goes straight into automated fulfillment and cannot be cancelled or changed, per OMNI’s policy. Cancel before the next renewal date to avoid one more shipment.

The OMNI Free Guarantee, read closely

  • It covers only your first subscription order, and only once.
  • You can get a refund for one opened pouch or box, based on the price you paid for it. Extra unopened items follow the normal return rules.
  • It does not cover subscription renewals, later orders, returning customers or one-time purchases.
  • The request must go through omni.loopreturns.com within 30 days of delivery.

So if you are on your second or third shipment and have opened it, the guarantee no longer applies. Cancel first, then return any unopened pouches within 30 days.

Charged after you cancelled?

OMNI’s policy treats a charge made after you cancelled, or a charge you did not authorize, separately from returns: email support@omnicreatine.com and they review it. To make that fast:

  • Write from the same email as the original order; OMNI says refund requests from other addresses are not processed.
  • Include the order number, the charge date and amount, and your cancellation screenshot.
  • Keep everything in one email thread; OMNI says duplicate messages slow responses.
  • If you get no answer, you can dispute the charge with your card issuer. Your cancellation proof is what they will ask for.

Lookalike sites to avoid

OMNI’s refund policy applies only to orders placed on omnicreatine.com. When we searched for how to cancel on November 24, 2026, Bing also showed an omnicreatine.cam page listing a support email on an unrelated domain, and our OMNI review found a second storefront at omniicreatine.com (double “i”) with different flavors and prices. We cannot tell who runs those.

Only use these: omnicreatine.com, portal.omnicreatine.com, omni.loopreturns.com and support@omnicreatine.com. Never give card details to a “support” page you reached from an ad or a phone number in search results. If you bought from a different storefront or marketplace, cancel through that seller’s account; OMNI’s policy does not cover it.

Questions

How do I cancel my OMNI Creatine subscription?

Go to portal.omnicreatine.com, sign in with the email on your order, open the active subscription and choose cancel. OMNI's refund policy (updated November 4, 2026) says no reason is needed and the cancellation takes effect right away, so you are not charged again. If the portal does not work, email support@omnicreatine.com from the address on your order; OMNI says it will make the change within 1 business day.

Can I get a refund on OMNI creatine gummies?

Unopened, sealed products can be returned within 30 days of delivery through omni.loopreturns.com, using your order number and shipping ZIP code. Opened products are refundable only under the OMNI Free Guarantee, which covers one opened pouch or box from your first subscription order, once. Renewals and one-time purchases are not covered for opened product.

I was charged after I cancelled OMNI. What do I do?

OMNI's policy says a charge made after you cancelled, or a charge you did not authorize, is handled outside the returns portal: email support@omnicreatine.com from your order address with the order number and the date you cancelled. Keep a screenshot of the cancellation. If you get no answer, your card issuer can dispute the charge.

Can I cancel an OMNI order that was just placed?

No. OMNI's policy says orders cannot be cancelled or changed once placed because they go straight into automated fulfillment. You can cancel the subscription so no further orders are created, then return the unopened order within 30 days of delivery.
