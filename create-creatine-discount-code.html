The cheapest route is not the gummies

If your goal is the lowest cost per gram of creatine and you are indifferent to format, the unflavoured stick packs are roughly $0.85 per 5 g against the gummy tub's $1.42 per 5 g — about 40% less. We set out the full comparison in the cheapest way to buy Create.

Most people buying gummies are not indifferent to format, and that is a legitimate position: the reason gummies exist is that people actually take them. Paying 57 cents more per dose for something you will not skip is a rational trade. Just make it knowingly.

Longer commitments do not beat Subscribe & Save

Create no longer sells separate multi-month bundles, and the “ships every 1 to 6 months” option under Subscribe & Save is not a bundle discount either — we tested every setting on both sizes and the price never changed. The only thing that lowers the price is choosing Subscribe & Save over a one-time purchase. The full test is laid out in our bundle analysis.

The practical consequence: subscribe, apply the 20%, and pick whichever shipping interval matches how fast you actually go through a tub or pouch — the interval itself won't change what you pay.

Coupon claims that do not work

Search results for Create discount codes are dominated by coupon-aggregator sites that generate pages for every brand whether or not they have a working code. The patterns to ignore:

  • Stacked percentages. "40% off with code X" almost always means 20% off subscription, restated as if the subscription discount and the code were two separate savings. They are the same $48 → about $40.80 step.
  • Expired first-order codes. Welcome offers from previous campaigns are scraped and republished indefinitely. They fail silently at checkout.
  • "Exclusive" codes with no link. If a page gives you a code to type but no link that carries it, the code is usually a guess.
  • Sitewide codes above 20%. We have not seen one hold. If you find one that does, it is worth more than ours and you should use it.

What to check before you click buy

  1. Confirm the discount is showing in the cart, not just promised on the page. It should appear as a line item.
  2. Check whether you are on subscribe or one-time. The 20% stacks on subscription; on a one-time order you are starting from the higher list price.
  3. Check the serving count, not the gummy count. A 90-count tub is 30 servings because a serving is three gummies. Pouches count sachets. Both work out to a month.
  4. Check current pricing on the product page. Create has changed its lineup and its prices more than once this year; we re-check ours monthly and date every figure, but the brand's own page is the authority at the moment you buy.
