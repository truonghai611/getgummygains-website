Questions

Does more creatine per serving mean better results?

Not necessarily. Most creatine research supports 3–5g/day as sufficient for muscle saturation; going above that range has not been shown to add proportional benefit for most users. A 6g serving is not wrong, but it is not automatically "better" than a verified 4.5–5g serving.

Is Bounce Nutrition NSF Certified for Sport?

We could not confirm a product-level NSF Certified for Sport registry listing for this specific product at time of writing. The brand markets third-party testing, but search the NSF database yourself for the exact SKU and flavor before relying on certification as a deciding factor.

How many gummies is a full Bounce Nutrition serving?

3 gummies, at 2g of creatine monohydrate each, for 6g total per the brand's current label — confirm this matches the exact package and flavor you are purchasing, since counts have varied across retail listings.
