Lab-result badges

If your creatine gummy met its label claim in a public independent lab round, you can show that on your product page with a badge that links to the source data.

By Henry Nguyen · Checked November 23, 2026 · How we evaluate

Rules for using a badge

  • A badge reports a result that a lab published. GummyGains did not test these products, and no brand pays for a badge or a listing.
  • A result applies to the lot the lab tested, in the year shown. A brand with mixed results gets a badge that says so.
  • Keep the link to the Creatine Gummy Lab Tracker so shoppers can check the row and the lab's own source.
  • If a newer public round reports a failure, the badge file changes to show it. Ask us at the address on the About page if you have a newer independent result.
