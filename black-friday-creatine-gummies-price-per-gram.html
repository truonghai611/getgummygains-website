The rule: divide, then compare

  1. Find total grams. Grams per serving × servings. Create: 4.5 g × 30 = 135 g.
  2. Divide the price by total grams. $60 / 135 g = $0.44 per gram.
  3. A deal has to land below $0.36 per gram to beat Create’s tub with the referral offer, or below $0.44 to beat today’s list price.
  4. Check the lab result, not only the label. A cheap gram that is not in the jar costs more. 16 of 28 gummy tests across three public rounds missed their label claim; Create (4.59 g against 4.5 g) and Force Factor (6.3 g against 5 g) passed SuppCo in 2025, single lots. See the lab tracker.
