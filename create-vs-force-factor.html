The case for Force Factor

It is the cheapest way to get a full 5 g of NSF Certified for Sport creatine in gummy form, and that is a genuinely useful thing to be. Roughly a dollar a dose puts it about 30% under Create on a per-gram basis. Six flavours are individually listed in the public NSF registry, so a tested athlete can buy it with confidence provided they check the specific flavour.

It is also the one brand in this category you can reasonably expect to find in a physical store rather than waiting for a delivery, which matters more than people admit — the main failure mode with creatine is not taking it, and running out because a subscription lapsed is a common reason.

The case for Create

Two things Force Factor does not have.

Depth of certification. Twenty-five Create flavours appear individually in the NSF Certified for Sport registry, against Force Factor's six. Certification is granted flavour by flavour, so this is not a vanity number: it means you are far less likely to pick a Create flavour that falls outside the certified list.

An independent measurement. Create appears in a published ISO 17025 assay that measured 4.59 g of creatine against a 4.5 g label — the only gummy in our set with a recent independent number that came in above its own claim. Force Factor has no published independent assay we could find. In a category where four of six products failed one independent round, that gap is the single strongest argument for paying more.

The dose difference is smaller than it looks

Force Factor's 5 g against Create's 4.5 g is a real 11% advantage on paper. In practice, the maintenance dose research supports roughly 3–5 g daily, and 4.5 g sits comfortably inside that band. Neither product is underdosing you. The number that actually differs in a way you can feel is the price, not the gram count.

How to decide

  • Cost per dose is the deciding factor. Force Factor. It is about 30% cheaper per gram and the NSF listing is real.
  • You want the best-documented product. Create. The ISO 17025 result is the only independent measurement in the category that came back above label.
  • You are drug-tested. Either, but check your exact flavour in the registry. Create simply has more certified flavours to choose from.
  • You want to buy it in a shop today. Force Factor. Create is direct-to-consumer.
  • You want sugar-free. Neither — see the sugar-free comparison.
