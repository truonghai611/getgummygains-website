Bear Balanced creatine gummies review

The brand that calls itself the world's first creatine gummy, built on Creapure, genuinely sugar-free, and the only one in our set that states gluten-free. It is also the lowest-dosed product we track and the most expensive per gram of creatine.

The headline number: Bear Balanced carries 3.5 g of creatine per three-gummy serving — the lowest of the nine brands we track, against a 5 g research standard. At $60 for a 30-serving container that is $2.00 a serving, or about $2.86 per 5 g, the highest per-gram price in the category. The formula is not plain creatine. Bear Balanced adds what it calls Creatine+™, which includes L-theanine and L-tyrosine alongside the Creapure creatine — positioned around focus and mental performance rather than strength alone.

What it gets right, and it is not nothing

Three things stand out against the rest of the category. It is the only genuinely sugar-free gummy we have found among the nine brands we track — Create and Beast Bites both list sugar in their panels. It is the only one that states gluten-free on the product page, which matters if your reason is coeliac disease rather than preference. And it uses Creapure, the German-made creatine monohydrate with the longest quality record, which it shares with Create.

The brand also has a real claim to having created the format. That is history rather than evidence, but it is not marketing invention.

The dose is the problem

3.5 g sits below the 5 g used in most creatine research, and below the 3–5 g range the ISSN describes. It is not a useless dose — saturation is a function of total daily intake over weeks rather than any single serving — but it means the label serving does not get you to the number the studies used.

The practical fix is four gummies instead of three, which gives you 4.67 g. That is a reasonable place to land. It also means a 90-gummy container lasts 22 days rather than 30, which moves the real cost from $2.00 a serving to roughly $2.67.

And there is no third-party product listing. We searched the NSF Certified for Sport registry for Bear Balanced directly and it returns nothing. The brand did not appear in the NOW Foods, SuppCo or James Smith/Eurofins independent rounds either. Creapure is a genuine mark of raw-material quality, but it certifies the ingredient the brand buys — not the finished gummy, its dose accuracy, or its stability on the shelf.

Our honest read

If you need sugar-free and gluten-free stated on the pack, Bear Balanced is currently the only creatine gummy in this category that gives you both, and that is a real reason to buy it. Go in knowing two things: take four gummies rather than three to reach a studied dose, and accept that you are relying on the brand's own account of what is in the product, because no independent lab result and no certification listing exists for it.

If those constraints are preferences rather than requirements, the economics are hard to defend. At the standard price you are paying roughly double per gram of creatine what a certified 5 g product costs.

Questions

How much creatine is in Bear Balanced gummies?

3.5 g of Creapure creatine monohydrate per three-gummy serving, brand-stated. That is the lowest per-serving dose of the nine creatine gummy brands we track, and below the 5 g used in most research. Four gummies gives you about 4.67 g.

Are Bear Balanced gummies sugar-free?

Yes, the brand states a sugar-free formula, and it is the only one of the nine brands we track that does. It also states vegan, gluten-free and keto-friendly. Create and Beast Bites both list sugar in their published ingredient panels.

Is Bear Balanced NSF Certified for Sport?

No. A direct search of the NSF Certified for Sport registry returns no Bear Balanced listing (checked 13 September 2026), and the brand did not appear in the NOW Foods, SuppCo or James Smith/Eurofins independent testing rounds. It does use Creapure, which is a raw-material quality mark rather than a finished-product certification.

What is Creatine+ in Bear Balanced?

The brand's proprietary blend, which adds L-theanine and L-tyrosine alongside the Creapure creatine monohydrate. Both are positioned around calm focus and mental performance rather than strength. The creatine content of the serving remains 3.5 g.

How much does Bear Balanced cost per serving?

$60 for a 90-gummy container of 30 servings, so $2.00 a serving at the standard price, falling to about $1.36 on a four-month order. Because the serving is 3.5 g rather than 5 g, the comparable figure is roughly $2.86 per 5 g of creatine — the highest in the category.

Bear Balanced or Create?

Bear Balanced if sugar-free or gluten-free is a requirement rather than a preference — it is the only option in this set that states both.
