Questions

Is Force Factor Creatine Gummies actually NSF Certified for Sport?

Yes, per brand and retail packaging. Still worth a quick check: search the NSF Certified for Sport database for the exact flavor (Grape, Watermelon, Orange, or Lemon-Lime) you are buying to confirm the specific SKU is listed, since certification is issued per product.

How does Force Factor compare to Create on price?

Force Factor is meaningfully cheaper per serving (~$1.00 vs. Create's ~$1.67–2.47), while both carry confirmed NSF Certified for Sport status. The main differences are piece count (5 gummies vs. 3) and Create's additional published independent lab measurement.

What does AstraGin do?

AstraGin is a proprietary ginseng-and-astragalus extract blend marketed for supporting nutrient absorption. Its research is supplier-funded — treat it as a possible bonus, not a reason to prioritize this product over a verified creatine dose alone.
