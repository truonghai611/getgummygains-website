Momentous Creatine Chews review

Momentous launched Creatine Chews in October 2025, and it is the clearest NSF Certified for Sport option we have reviewed in this category — with one catch worth knowing before you buy: it is not really a gummy.

The numbers

  • Dose: 5 g of Creapure-branded creatine monohydrate per serving, delivered as 5 chews at 1 g each.
  • Certification: NSF Certified for Sport — a named, searchable registry, which puts Momentous ahead of most competitors we've reviewed in this category on verification alone.
  • Format: 150-count jar, 30 servings.
  • Price: around $49.95 per jar — roughly $1.67 per 5 g dose, in the same range as most of the branded gummies in this series despite the stronger certification.
  • Other label claims: vegan, gluten-free, non-GMO, no artificial dyes.
  • Flavors: Lemon Lime and Mango.

Chew, not gummy — and that distinction matters

This is the detail most roundups skip. Momentous markets this product as a "chew," and major retail listings describe it as a chewable tablet, not a gummy candy. That is a real manufacturing difference, not just marketing language: gummies are cast from a gelatin or pectin base with water and heat, which is the exact process that degrades creatine into creatinine over time and is the root cause of most of the underdosing problems documented in independent lab testing of gummy-format products. A pressed or compressed chewable tablet does not go through that same water-and-heat casting process, which may make it inherently more stable — though we have not seen independent lab data confirming shelf-life performance for this specific product.

Why this is worth knowing: if the reason you are considering a gummy-style product is candy-like taste and texture, a chew may not deliver that experience. If the reason is convenience and avoiding a scoop, a chew accomplishes the same goal with a manufacturing process that sidesteps the specific degradation mechanism gummies are prone to.

NSF Certified for Sport, without an independent lab result we can point to

NSF Certified for Sport is one of the two certification programs we consider meaningful in this category (Informed Sport is the other), because both maintain public, searchable registries. Momentous Creatine Chews carry this certification, which is a stronger verification signal than most products in our review series. That said, certification and independent third-party lab testing are two different things — NSF certification confirms the product was tested against NSF's standards, but this product does not appear in either of the two public lab-testing rounds (NOW Foods or SuppCo) we track independently for this category.

Our honest read. Momentous’s NSF Certified for Sport listing is real and searchable, and at roughly $1.67 per 5 g dose the price is competitive. Two things to weigh: it is a compressed chew rather than a gummy, and it has not appeared in either independent lab round we track — so the certification is the whole of the evidence. If you want a gummy that carries both the searchable certification and a published lab measurement, Create is currently the one.
