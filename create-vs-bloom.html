Why verification matters more for gummies

Across three public testing rounds, 16 of 28 gummy tests missed their label claim, some with almost no creatine. “Third-party verified” without a named program can’t be looked up; NSF Certified for Sport can, flavor by flavor. That is the main reason we rank a certified, tested gummy above an unverified one, even when the unverified one looks better on paper.

Where Bloom comes out ahead

  • Sugar-free label, a real difference if you watch sugar (Create has about 5 g per serving). See sugar-free creatine gummies.
  • Full 5 g per 3-gummy serving and a slightly lower price per 5 g.

Where Create comes out ahead

  • Named certification you can check in NSF’s directory.
  • Measured dose: 4.59 g against a 4.5 g label in SuppCo’s 2025 round, and a pass in the July 2025 Eurofins round.
  • 20% referral offer through our link, and 30-serving tubs.

Which should you buy?

If avoiding sugar is your top priority and you accept an unverified dose, Bloom. If you want a gummy whose dose and contaminant screening you can check, Create. If you want both sugar-free and verified, plain creatine monohydrate powder is the cheapest way.

Questions

Is Bloom or Create creatine better?

Bloom is sugar-free with a full 5 g, but we found no named certification or public potency test. Create has NSF Certified for Sport flavors and two published passing lab tests, with about 5 g of sugar per serving.

Are Bloom creatine gummies third-party tested?

Bloom says its gummies are third-party verified but does not name the program. We did not find them in NSF Certified for Sport or Informed Sport, or in the three public potency rounds we track.

Which has more sugar, Bloom or Create?

Bloom is labeled sugar-free. Create’s Blue Raspberry NSF record lists 5 g of total sugar per 3-gummy serving.

Which is cheaper per serving?

Bloom is about $1.67 per 5 g on the 12-serving bottle; Create is about $1.78 per 5 g on subscription. Create’s referral link advertises 20% off, which can change the comparison in your cart.
