Is Lemme Creatine Gummies NSF Certified for Sport?

Yes, per brand and retail listings. As with any certified product, search the NSF database for the exact flavor you're buying to confirm the specific SKU listing.

How much sugar is in a serving of Lemme Creatine Gummies?

4g of added sugar per serving, per the brand's current label — the highest among the gummy brands compared in this review series. Factor this in if you take multiple gummy supplements daily.

Is Lemme worth the higher price?
