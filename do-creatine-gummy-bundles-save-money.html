Why the frequency selector isn't a bundle

Create used to sell separate multi-month bundle packs — fixed multi-tub and multi-pouch packs priced as one-off purchases with their own discount, which is what this page originally compared against a plain monthly subscription. As of 18 September 2026 we could not find those bundle listings anywhere on the site. What replaced them is a single product page with a subscribe-or-one-time toggle, and a “ships every 1 to 6 months” dropdown nested under the subscribe option.

That dropdown looks like a commitment-length discount — the kind where buying further ahead earns a better rate. It is not one. It only changes how often Create ships and bills you, not what you pay per shipment.

What choosing a longer shipping interval does buy you

It is not pointless, even though it doesn't change the price. Two real reasons to pick a longer interval anyway:

  • One fewer thing to think about. A 6-month interval means five fewer billing and shipping events a year than the 1-month setting, for people who would rather not touch the account each month.
  • It doesn't cost anything to choose. Since the price is the same at every setting, there's no financial reason to pick 1-month over 6-month or vice versa — pick whichever matches how fast you actually go through a tub or pouch, and how far ahead you're comfortable committing given the cancellation policy below.

We could not confirm from the product page alone how many units are shipped on a longer interval — whether a 6-month setting ships one month's supply every six months or more at once. Check what's actually in your cart before you pay.

How to check this for any brand

Divide the shipment price by the number of full servings it contains, not by the gummy count or the number on the label. Then compare subscribing against a one-time order on the same size. Separately, test whether any “buy for longer” option actually changes the price shown, or only the delivery timing — the two are easy to mix up, and this page is a case in point.

Why is the pouch more expensive than the tub for the same number of servings?

Packaging. A pouch is 30 individually portioned sachets; a tub is loose gummies you portion yourself. Both give you 30 servings a month, but the tub runs about 17% cheaper per serving.

Does choosing a longer shipping interval cost more or less?

Neither, as far as we could tell. We selected every interval from 1 to 6 months on both sizes on 18 September 2026 and the price shown at checkout stayed the same. Is there any risk in subscribing?

The one to know about: Create's policy states that cancelling a subscription does not cancel an order that has already been processed, and orders typically process within one business day of the reminder email. Treat the reminder email as the deadline for changes, not the renewal date.

Do these numbers apply to other brands?

The method does; the exact numbers do not.
