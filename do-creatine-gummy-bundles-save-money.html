The comparison that changes the answer

Five one-month subscriptions at $48 would cost $240 for 150 servings if that price stayed constant. A High Five subscription displayed at about $199 supplies the same serving count for about $41 less, roughly 17% lower. It also requires a larger upfront payment. The $249 one-time High Five option is a different comparison: about $1.66 per serving, versus $2.00 for a $60 one-time tub.

This is not a ranking of every bundle. We checked the options above. We have not rechecked every flavor, pouch bundle or checkout promotion, and the old eleven-bundle table has been removed.

Choose based on how you actually buy

  • Trying a flavor for the first time: a smaller order reduces the amount committed before you know you like it.
  • Already know you will use the product: compare the multi-month subscription with repeated one-month purchases, including renewal prices and delivery frequency.
  • Do not want recurring orders: compare one-time prices with one-time prices; a subscription saving creates an ongoing order arrangement.
  • Lowest creatine cost matters most: include plain powder in your comparison. Gummies charge for convenience and taste.

Check the total before paying

  1. Confirm tub versus pouch, flavor and serving count. A tub's count is gummies; pouch counts may refer to sachets.
  2. Confirm one-time versus subscription, delivery frequency and the next renewal price.
  3. Read the actual discount line, shipping and tax. Do not deduct another 20% from this table unless the cart applies it to your selection.
  4. Divide the final total by labeled servings. For products with different serving amounts, also compare cost per gram.

Questions

Do Create bundles save money compared with a monthly subscription?

Some bundle subscriptions can. On September 18, 2026, the High Five tub subscription displayed about $199 for 150 servings, compared with $240 for five $48 monthly tubs. That is approximately 17% less before additional discounts, shipping and tax.

Does the affiliate offer stack with subscriptions and bundles?

We have not verified stacking at checkout. Check whether the discount applies to your selected product and purchase type before paying; do not assume an extra 20% deduction.

Is a 450-count tub bundle 450 servings?

No. At three gummies per labeled serving, 450 gummies provide 150 servings. Check whether a count refers to individual gummies or pre-portioned sachets.

Should I buy the largest bundle?

Only if its total cost, flavors, serving count and subscription terms fit your needs. A lower cost per serving still means a larger upfront payment and more product to use.
