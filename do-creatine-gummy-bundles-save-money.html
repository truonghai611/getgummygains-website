Why the bundle loses to the subscription

Because they are two different discounts and they do not stack the way shoppers assume. The subscription discount is 20% off the list price of a single box, applied every month for as long as you stay subscribed. A bundle discount is a one-off reduction on a larger cart — and on Create's tub bundles that reduction lands at between 11% and 17%, which is less than 20%.

Put plainly: a bundle is a volume discount that is smaller than the loyalty discount sitting next to it. The bundles are aimed at people who do not want a subscription, not at people trying to get the lowest price.

The two things a bundle does buy you

This is not an argument that bundles are a trick. Two real advantages:

  • Flavour variety in one order. Most of Create's bundles are multi-flavour by design — Starter, Extreme Sour Trio, Rocket Pop Duo. A single-flavour subscription cannot do that without juggling several subscriptions.
  • No recurring charge to manage. Create's own terms say cancelling does not stop an order that has already processed, and changes must be made before the reminder email's cut-off. A bundle is one charge with nothing to remember.

If either of those matters more to you than five cents a serving, buy the bundle. Just buy it knowing that is the trade, rather than because the page says Save.

How to check this for any brand

Divide the bundle price by the number of full servings it contains, not by the number of gummies or the number of months on the label. Then do the same for a single box on subscription. Two-thirds of the time in this category the subscription wins, because subscription discounts are set by marketing teams chasing retention and bundle discounts are set to clear inventory.

Bottom line. At Create, subscribe to the 90-count tub. It is cheaper per serving than all eleven bundles, it ships free, and you are not sitting on six months of a flavour you might get bored of. Buy a bundle only for the flavour variety or to avoid a recurring charge.

The 20% from our link applies either way — to a bundle, a one-time box or a subscription. If you are subscribing, it stacks on top of the subscription price, which is how the 90-count tub gets to $38.40.

Questions

Do Create's bundles save money compared to subscribing?

No. Through our link the best tub bundles work out to $1.33 a serving, while subscribing to the 90-count tub is $1.28. The bundle discounts land between roughly 11% and 17%, which is less than the 20% subscription discount they are competing with.

Which Create bundle is the best value if I want one anyway?

The five-month, 450-count tub bundle and the six-month, 540-count tub bundle, both at about $1.33 a serving through our link. Avoid the two-month tub bundle at $1.53 and all of the pouch bundles, which run from $1.44 to $1.87.

Why are the pouch bundles so much more expensive?

Because a pouch is a single-serve sachet and a tub is loose gummies, so a 60-count pouch bundle is sixty servings while a 180-count tub bundle is also sixty servings — at a much lower price. You are paying for thirty pieces of packaging a month.

Does the 20% affiliate discount work on bundles?

Yes. It is an order-level discount, so it applies to bundles, one-time boxes and subscriptions alike. On a subscription it stacks on top of the subscription price, which is why the 90-count tub lands near $38.40 rather than $48.

Is there any risk in subscribing?

The one to know about: Create's policy states that cancelling a subscription does not cancel an order that has already been processed, and orders typically process within one business day of the reminder email. Treat the reminder email as the deadline for changes, not the renewal date.

Do these numbers apply to other brands?

The method does; the answer does not. Divide the bundle price by full servings and compare it against a single box on that brand's own subscription discount. Whether the bundle or the subscription wins depends entirely on how the two discounts are set, and brands rarely set them to be consistent with each other.
