Is the Create sold in stores the same product?

Retail tubs we checked list the same 1.5 g creatine monohydrate per gummy and 3-gummy serving as the direct product, and Target’s variety listing names NSF Certified for Sport. Certification is listed by flavor, so match the flavor you buy in the NSF directory. Gummies are heat- and moisture-sensitive; avoid tubs that look sticky or clumped and check expiry dates.

Avoiding fakes and grey-market listings

  1. On Amazon and Walmart, buy only when the seller is Create Wellness.
  2. Be wary of eBay or marketplace listings far below $35 for 90 gummies.
  3. Check that the lot number and best-by date are printed, and that the seal is intact.
