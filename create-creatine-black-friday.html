How to judge a Black Friday creatine deal

  1. Convert to price per serving. A serving is 3 Create gummies (4.5 g creatine). Use our calculator for any brand.
  2. Compare against the 3-month subscription (about $1.48 per serving). Anything above that is not a deal for a regular user.
  3. Check what renews. Holiday subscription discounts sometimes apply to the first order only. Read the renewal price before you check out.
  4. Do not overbuy. Gummies have a shorter shelf life than powder and are sensitive to heat and humidity. Buy what you will use before the best-by date. See do creatine gummies expire?
  5. Bundles are not always cheaper. See do Create bundles save money?
