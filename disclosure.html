How this site makes money

GummyGains is reader-supported. Some outbound links to products are affiliate links, which means if you click one and buy something, we may receive a commission from the retailer or brand. The price you pay is the same either way.

What this does not change

  • Which products we cover. Products are selected against our published criteria, not by commission rate.
  • What we say about them. Every product we recommend has a "worth knowing" section listing its actual weaknesses, and we publish lab results that complicate our own recommendations.
  • Ordering. Ranking follows the criteria. No brand has ever paid for position, and no brand reviews a page before publication.

Which links are affiliate links

Any button or link that sends you to a retailer or brand storefront to check a price should be assumed to be an affiliate link. These are marked with rel="sponsored" in the page source, and every page carrying them shows a disclosure above the first one.

Links to lab results, research bodies, testing programmes and other reference sources are never affiliate links.
