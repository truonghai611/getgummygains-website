Where Beast Bites genuinely wins

On arithmetic alone, Beast Bites is the better buy. Subscribed to the 150-count pouch you are paying $1.20 for a full 5 g dose. That is the cheapest certified gummy dose we have priced anywhere, and it undercuts Create's best reachable price by about 15% per gram. It also gives you the whole 5 g in one serving rather than 4.5 g, so nobody has to think about whether the dose is "close enough".

The eight NSF Certified for Sport flavour listings are real and verifiable in the public registry — more flavour-level entries than Force Factor or Lemme carry. For a tested athlete under a banned-substance policy, that certification is the thing that matters, and Beast Bites has it.

Where the Beast Bites case weakens

An independent HPLC round run by NOW Foods listed Beast Bites among products that did not meet label claim, with elevated creatinine. Creatinine is what creatine degrades into, so an elevated figure points at either a manufacturing problem or heat and moisture exposure. Create and Con-Cret appeared in the same round. The difference is what happened next: Create appears in two later published rounds and passed both, including the ISO 17025 assay that measured 4.59 g. Beast Bites does not appear in either. There is no published independent measurement of a current Beast Bites batch confirming or clearing the earlier result.

NSF Certified for Sport is not the same test. It verifies that a product does not contain banned substances and is made under audited conditions; it is not a per-batch potency assay published for shoppers to read. A brand can be legitimately certified and still have an open question about how much creatine survives to the end of shelf life. That is the gap here.

How to decide in one question

Ask yourself which risk you would rather carry.

  • You care most about cost per gram and getting a full 5 g. Buy Beast Bites. You will pay roughly 15% less per gram of creatine, and the certification is genuine.
  • You care most about the published testing record. Buy Create. It is the only gummy in our set with a recent, independent, ISO 17025 number that came in above its own label.
  • You are a tested athlete. Either works — both are in the NSF registry. Check the specific flavour you are buying, because certification is granted flavour by flavour, not brand-wide.
  • You want sugar-free. Neither. Both contain cane sugar. Our sugar-free comparison covers what is left.

Does Beast Bites have more creatine than Create?

Per serving, yes: 5 g against Create's 4.5 g, both brand-stated. The relevant caveat is that Create's 4.5 g label has been independently checked and measured at 4.59 g, while the only published independent measurement of Beast Bites flagged it for not meeting claim with elevated creatinine.

Why was Beast Bites flagged in a lab test?

An independent HPLC round commissioned by NOW Foods listed it among products not meeting label claim, with elevated creatinine — the compound creatine degrades into. Create and Con-Cret were flagged in the same round. Create appears in two later published rounds and passed both; Beast Bites does not appear in either, so the result stands unrevisited rather than confirmed.
